The trillion-dollar trust gap

The science is brutal on carbon markets

Peer-reviewed studies keep finding the same thing: most credits don't represent real carbon. The data going onto the ledger was never trustworthy in the first place.

94%

of forest-carbon credits were phantom

A 2023 study in Science of 89 million credits found only ~6% backed by real, additional carbon. The other 94% are tonnes someone paid to remove — still in the air.

West et al., Science 2023 · doi:10.1126/science.ade3535
19%

of REDD+ projects met their targets

A 2025 Science analysis of 52 REDD+ projects found just 19% delivered the emission reductions they claimed. Verification, not ledgers, is the bottleneck.

Tang et al., Science 2025 · doi:10.1126/science.adw4094
Why everyone else is solving the wrong half

A blockchain only proves a number didn't change — not that it was ever true

01 · THE TRAP

Blockchain makes bad data permanent

Put an unverified credit on-chain and all you've done is make a bad number immutable and tradeable. Transparency about garbage is still garbage.

02 · THE REAL GAP

The oracle problem

The hard part is getting a trustworthy measurement onto the chain in the first place. That's a verification problem, and it's where every registry fails.

03 · THE FIX

One primitive: the Verified Climate Claim

AI-verified from independent open data, anchored with an evidence hash, made globally unique by a geo-temporal fingerprint. Verify once, trust everywhere.

    Biomass → additional CO₂e
